    Son, Bouanga score 1st-half goals as LAFC beats Real Salt Lake

    Son Heung-min scores for the second consecutive game, Denis Bouanga adds a goal in the 40th minute and LAFC prevails, 3-1. Goalkeeper Hugo Lloris falls a few minutes short of another shutout.

    LOS ANGELES — Following up his first goal of the MLS season, which punctuated a lopsided victory over the rival Galaxy last week, Son Heung-min celebrated his second bobblehead night of the month in style.

    Less than two weeks after being honored by the Dodgers with a crossover collectible, Son’s terrific finish in the 11th minute on Wednesday night propelled Los Angeles FC to a 3-1 victory over Real Salt Lake at BMO Stadium.

    Scoring gave the 34-year-old South Korean a chance to strike his famous picture-taking pose – which had been immortalized on the 22,000 bobbleheads passed out to ticketholders prior to LAFC’s first home match since May 24.

    Son’s breakthrough came when Nkosi Tafari cleared the RSL lines with a pass over the top to winger Jacob Shaffelburg. The Canadian’s perfect touch 40 yards away from the Salt Lake net freed Son, who drove toward the top of the box, stepped over the ball and struck a low shot with his left foot.

    Before exiting in the 72nd minute, Son also helped deliver LAFC’s third goal when his attempted cross was redirected off defender DeAndre Yedlin for an own-goal past RSL goalkeeper Rafael Cabral.

    Unable to score in league play through 13 appearances prior to the seven-week World Cup break, Son’s contributions in back-to-back wins have helped LAFC (9-5-3, 30 points) move closer to the top of the Western Conference standings.

    Now two points behind San Jose (10-4-2) and Vancouver (10-3-2), both losers in their respective midweek matches, LAFC forward Denis Bouanga said his team needs “to push again a little bit to catch the teams in front of us.”

    LAFC can tally nine points in as many days when it hosts last-place Sporting Kansas City (4-10-2, 14 points) on Saturday night.

    Bouanga also scored in consecutive contests.

    “It’s good for the confidence,” Bouanga said. “For the team, for me, and Sonny, too.”

    Bouanga’s game-winner in the 40th minute was the product of one of LAFC’s best team goals this year.

    Operating inside its own half, the Black & Gold worked the ball back and forth until Canadian midfielder Mathieu Choinière sprung the French forward with an excellent through ball. Son earned an assist on the play as well for his MLS-best 10th of the season.

    Bouanga did the rest, dribbling into the box before smashing his eighth goal of the regular season.

    “I want to see that more,” LAFC head coach Marc Dos Santos said. “I want to see us having the courage to play in between lines. When a team is pressuring us to find this extra third man in between lines, because when we do that, that’s when we’re really good also.”

    Choinière, like his Canadian teammate Shaffelburg, returned from the World Cup fit and in form.

    “Of course it gave me confidence to play at the World Cup,” Choinière said. “Playing at the highest stage, it was amazing.”

    Juggling injuries and outbound departures during the recently-opened MLS secondary transfer window – prior to the game LAFC announced that Swedish U22 signing Amin Boudri was leaving the group – Dos Santos watched his players dictate terms for the second match in a matter of days.

    Buoyed by midfielder Diego Luna and 19-year-old attacker Zavier Gozo, RSL (8-5-2, 26 points) came into the match one spot behind LAFC in the conference standings with two games in hand.

    Gozo is the first Utah native to be named to the MLS All-Star first XI, and the youngest RSL player to receive the honor, but on Wednesday he and his teammates were held in check.

    LAFC’s four-man back line, thanks especially to fullbacks Yevhen Cheberko and Ryan Hollingshead, repeated a quality effort coming off the Galaxy performance.

    Utilizing speedy wingbacks who like to stretch opponents, RSL is best defended by five at the back. But that is not how LAFC prefers to play under Dos Santos.

    “Against teams like that we demand a lot of shifting from our back four,” Dos Santos said. “Both Ryan and Cheba were very committed.”

    An RSL goal in the 86th minute by defender Lukas Engel cost LAFC its 11th clean sheet in 17 league games, as well as goalkeeper Hugo Lloris’ 10th shutout by the midway point of their league schedule.

    Lloris made six saves, equaling his season high.

    Engel’s shot, after a corner kick, glanced off Tafari, who started in place of center back Aaron Long – the lone change from the starting lineup in Carson.

    “The team looked great,” Choinière said. “Everybody mentally looks very fresh and ready to compete. We all know that we can play the top of the league, so that’s what we’re trying to do.”
