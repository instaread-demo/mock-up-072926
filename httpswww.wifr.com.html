Teens from Woodstock, Crystal Lake among 4 identified in fatal Chicago crash

By Jason Near
Published: Jul. 27, 2026 at 12:46 PM EDT

CHICAGO, Ill. (WIFR) - Four teens, three of whom were from McHenry County, died when a car drove off a bridge and crashed into a train during a police pursuit Sunday morning in Chicago, investigators report.

The Cook County Medical Examiner identified the teens as Yuarem Garcia-Pena, 18, of Woodstock; Cristofer Lopez Montan, 19, of Woodstock; Yaritza Martinez Villeda, 17, of Crystal Lake; and Vanessa Morales, 14, of Palatine.

Chicago police said the incident stemmed from a report of someone pointing a gun at a driver, according to CBS Chicago. Police tried to pull over a vehicle that matched the caller’s description, but it did not stop. Officers chased the vehicle briefly before ending the pursuit.

Illinois State Police reportedly found the same vehicle in the 5500 block of Cicero Avenue. Police say the driver stopped, but fled immediately after.

As the car sped away, it left the road in the 6900 block of S. Cicero Avenue, went off a bridge and hit a train car.

Three teens died at the scene and the fourth died at a nearby hospital.

The incident remains under investigation.
