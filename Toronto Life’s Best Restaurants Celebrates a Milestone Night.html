Toronto Life’s Best Restaurants Celebrates a Milestone Night

On Monday, June 8, 2026, Toronto’s culinary community gathered at Evergreen Brick Works for a monumental milestone: the 10th-anniversary edition of Toronto Life’s Best Restaurants presented by Uber Eats. The sold-out event delivered its biggest experience yet for food fans, doubling as a milestone celebration for Toronto Life’s 60th year of shaping the city's cultural landscape.

Produced by Wonder, SJC Media's experiential marketing pillar, the evening showcased how editorial brands can be brought to life through unforgettable consumer experiences and premium brand partnerships. As Toronto Life’s signature culinary event, the immersive tasting experience welcomed more than 1,200 guests for a one-night-only celebration. The evening celebrated the iconic chefs, breakout talents and industry leaders shaping Toronto's culinary scene today.

A Look Inside the Celebration

The Ultimate Tasting Experience
Guests flowed through the historic venue to sample standout dishes from more than 20 of the top-tier establishments featured in Toronto Life’s annual Where to Eat Now issue.

The Stella Artois Perfect Serve Awards
The evening began with the Stella Artois Perfect Serve Awards, where 300 VIP guests watched Canada's top bartenders compete for the National Draught Master title and a chance to represent Canada at the Global Finals. The competition crowned Anthony Saucier of Shaker Cuisine & Mixologie, while guests enjoyed passed appetizers from Bar Eugenie and Cheese Boutique.

The Tricon VIP Lounge
Introducing a new event experience, Tricon welcomed residents and guests alike to a premium, exclusive lounge experience, complete with a playful "Ring to Celebrate" champagne station.

The Uber Eats Bookends
Celebrating its own 10-year anniversary, presenting sponsor Uber Eats curated the "First and Last Bite" experiences, greeting guests with signature welcome Jell-O bites from Barcart and offering a sweet send-off courtesy of Craig’s Cookies.

What began a decade ago as a celebration of Toronto's best dining destinations has grown into one of the city's most anticipated culinary events, celebrating the chefs, restaurateurs and hospitality teams that make Toronto one of North America's most exciting food cities.
