Metro’s World Cup success

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:38
Instaread

Metro’s World Cup coverage has generated a whopping 16 million page views, doubling the audience size achieved during Euro 2024.

In what has been a remarkable summer of sport, Metro has delivered outstanding cross-platform results with more than 200 million views across Facebook, Instagram and Apple News.

James Goldman, Metro Head of Sport, said: “This has been an astonishing period for Metro Sport, beginning with Arsenal finally ending their wait to win the Premier League title, before suffering Champions League heartache, through to Wimbledon and culminating in record-breaking World Cup coverage.”

In The Mixer, the newsletter that brought football fans closer to the World Cup action, also saw strong growth.

Subscriber numbers increased by 62.5% since 14 May and 99% of readers who signed up during the preview campaign are still signed up.

Despite moving from a weekly newsletter to daily editions throughout the tournament, engagement has remained in line with the newsletter’s usual weekly performance, showing no signs of audience fatigue or declining reader interest.

Goldman added: “The success we’ve seen is no accident, but a continuation of the progress we’ve made over the last 12 months, especially since enrolling in the FT Strategies programme last summer which helped us better understand our audience and the content that they want to consume, as well as accelerating the integration between our print, digital and video operations.

“Ultimately, the results are down to the hard work, commitment and skill of a team which has gone above and beyond time and time again. The challenge now is to build from a position of strength so we can capitalise on our recent achievements.”
