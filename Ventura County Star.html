A 52-year-old motorcyclist was hospitalized following a crash with a car in Oxnard on July 24.

Officers with the Oxnard Police Department arrived at the intersection of Ventura Road and Doris Avenue around 6:23 a.m. and found a Harley-Davidson with major damage and its rider lying on the ground in the middle of the intersection, according to the police department news release.
