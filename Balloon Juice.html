Debate Night in Wisconsin – Dem Nomination for Governor is Totally Up for Grabs

 

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:58
Instaread

Tonight, the five candidates running in the Democratic Primary for Governor in Wisconsin will be debating.

But only for an hour. Starting at 7 PM CDT.

5 people for 55 minutes, tops, really means, at most, 10 minutes each.

And 50% of the Dems in WI haven’t decided, yet, how they want to vote in the August 11th primary. And WI has no run-off law. So, 35-40% snags you the nom.

Here is the Wisconsin TV station hosting the debate. Click to watch at 7:00

The candidates are:

Francesca Hong – Madison State Assembly Rep,  Unaffiliated DSA supporter

Mandela Barnes – Former State Rep, Former US Senate candidate, popular TV commenter

David Crowley – Recent entry, Milwaukee County Executive

Joel Brennan – Business guy, President of the Greater Milwaukee Committee

Kelda Roys. – former State Rep from Madison, former candidate for Governor

After each candidate expresses their justifiable concerns about the terrible storm damage that ravaged the state, and the recent horrific killing incident, not a lot of time to make their case.

Much less clear up the complicated backstory to the primary. Which we will not attempt now, either. Milwaukee Magazine gave it a shot: Candidates For Wisconsin Governor 2026

People in, people out, court weigh-ins, terrible quotes, and Gov Tony Evers with a last-minute endorsement of last-minute entrant David Crowley.

Trivia Man, who brought the debate to our attention, sends this advice to the entire field:

“Our candidates must appear reasonable and controlled. Message discipline has never been more important.”

This is because the GOP is waiting to pounce – as they already are – with bad quotes both past and present.

On, Wisconsin! May you find your way to victory for us all.

***

I’ve been listening to this podcast with Chris Kluwe, whom I’ve followed on Bluesky forever, but this was the first time I’d heard an in-depth conversation.
