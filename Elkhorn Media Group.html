JOHN DAY AND PRAIRIE CITY – (Press Release from the Malheur National Forest)

Effective Wednesday, July 29, 2026 at 12:01 am, the North Zone (Blue Mountain and Prairie City Ranger Districts) of the Malheur National Forest will implement Industrial Fire Precaution Level (IFPL) IV. This change is due to increasing temperatures, drier fuels and increased fire danger.

Under Industrial Fire Precaution Level (IFPL) IV: general shutdown, all operations are prohibited.

Both Zones of the Malheur National Forest remain in Public Use Restriction (PURs) Stage C and in EXTREME fire danger.

For additional information about Fire Danger, Public Use Restrictions, and Industrial Fire Precaution Levels, visit https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/malheur/fire/prevention.
