Lies! No H2O

0:00 / 2:05
Instaread

Today I achieved a new trail milestone. My first 30 mile day. I didn’t wake up with the intention to go 30 miles, but over the course of the day I made it happen.

Starting out with breakfast, I tried a new one of granola, freeze dried fruit, and powdered milk. All I had to do was add water and I had instant breakfast cereal! I will definitely be doing this again.


Our water carries were a little tough today. A lot of the streams are drying up, so we had some longer stretches between water. It wasn’t anything I couldn’t handle, just took a little more planning. We were hiking through a burn zone from the Dixie fire. It could have been a tough hike in full sun and heat, but today was a little grey and even rainy off and on. It was still hot and humid.


Drom, Posh, and I hiked together on and off all day. With Posh being from New Zealand, we’ve had a running joke about measuring all of our distances in meters, specifically the megameter (aka 1,000 kilometers). Today we passed 2 megameters on trail, so of course we had to create a marker on the side of the trail for the photo op.

We were hiking until about 8:30 pm. My feet were moving slowly and were absolutely feeling it. I quickly pitched my tent and didn’t move until morning.

I ate a Thai curry dinner plus a bunch of snacks and knocked out instantly. I’m so proud of us for our first 30. I didn’t think I could do it. Pretty cool.
